- What is Burp Suite
	- Background:
		- It is a Java-based framework designed to serve as a solution for conducting web app penetration testing
		- Industry standard tool for hands-on security assessments of web/mobile apps (including APIs)
		- Captures & enables manipulation of all HTTP/HTTPS traffic between a browser and web server
		- Versions: Free community edition & paid professional edition & enterprise suite
- Features of Burp Community
	- Key Features
		- Proxy: The Burp Proxy is the most renowned aspect of Burp Suite. It enables interception and modification of requests and responses while interacting with web applications.
		- Repeater: allows for capturing, modifying, and resending the same request multiple times.
		- Intruder: allows for spraying endpoints with requests. It is commonly utilized for brute-force attacks or fuzzing endpoints.
		- Decoder: offers a valuable service for data transformation. It can decode captured information or encode payloads before sending them to the target.
		- Comparer: enables the comparison of two pieces of data at either the word or byte level.
		- Sequencer: typically employed when assessing the randomness of tokens, such as session cookie values or other supposedly randomly generated data.
