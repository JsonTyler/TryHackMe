- Introduction
	- A SOC (Security Operations Center) is a dedicated facility operated by a specialized security team
		- The team aims to continuously monitor an org's network & resources and identify suspicious activity to prevent damage
		- Works 24/7
- Purpose & Components
	- Main focus of the SOC team is to keep "Detection" & "Response" intact
		- via continuous monitoring and security solutions
	- Detection
		- **Detect vulnerabilities**:  Find weaknesses attackers could exploit, such as unpatched software.
		- **Detect unauthorized activity**: Identify unauthorized logins or account use using clues like location.
		- **Detect policy violations**: Identify actions that violate company security policies, such as insecurely sharing confidential files.
		- **Detect intrusions**: Detect unauthorized access to systems/networks, such as exploitation or malware infections.
	- Response
		- **Support with the incident response**: Once an incident is detected, certain steps are taken to respond to it. This response includes minimizing its impact and performing the root cause analysis of the incident. The SOC team also helps the incident response team carry out these steps.
	- 3 Pillars of a SOC (People, Process, Technology) coexist

- People
	- The `People` are known as the SOC team
		- **SOC Analyst L1:** Triage alerts and determine if they’re harmful.
		- **SOC Analyst L2:** Perform deeper investigations and correlate data.
		- **SOC Analyst L3:** Proactively hunt threats and support incident response.
		- **Security Engineer:** Deploy and configure security solutions.
		- **Detection Engineer:** Create and maintain detection rules.
		- **SOC Manager:** Manage the SOC and report to the CISO.
- Process
	- Each role on the SOC team has its own "Processes"
	- Alter Triage
		- Basis of the SOC team
		- First response to any alert is to performthe triage which is focused on analyzing the specific alert
			- Determines the severity of the alert & helps with prioritization
			- Answers the 5Ws
			- ![[Pasted image 20261004182809.png|246]]
	- Reporting
		- The detected harmful alerts will need to be escalation for response/solutions
			- Escalated as tickets and assigned
			- Report should discuss all the 5 Ws with a thorough analysis/screenshots for evidence
	- Incident Response & Forensics
		- If a reported detection is highly malicious and critical then a high level team initiates an incident repsonse
- Technology
	- Technology portion refers to the security solutionis which minimize the SOC team's manual effort to detect and respond to threats
	- Security Solutions help to centralize all the information of the devices/applicaitons present in the network to automate the detection/response capabilities
	- Some security solutions:
		- **SIEM:** Collects and correlates logs to **detect** suspicious activity.
		- **EDR:** Monitors endpoints in real time, detects threats, investigates, and can respond automatically.
		- **Firewall:** Filters network traffic and blocks unauthorized/suspicious connections.
		- **Other:** Antivirus, IDS/IPS, XDR, etc. may also be used depending on the organization’s needs.
